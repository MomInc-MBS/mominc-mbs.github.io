Mechanics

These are approved descriptions of the mechanics that run today. Numbers marked as draft or open can still change.

Minotaur haunt loop

  1. Prep. Doors closed, no monster. Players stage offerings.
  2. Round 1. Players deliver offerings to the tribute door. A delivery is a three-second hold, and another player must witness it from the same spot.
  3. Breather. The monster pauses and the shop is open.
  4. Round 2. The Minotaur makes charge passes in waves. Players sidestep and can stun him for a moment. He cannot be felled here.
  5. Breather. As above.
  6. Round 3: finale. The house locks. The group finishes the deliveries and lays a thread to the exit to win.

Win: enough true offerings delivered and the thread laid. Lose: every living player is down.

Players and safety

  • Up to 8 players. Solo play uses the Director as witness.
  • A dropped phone can rejoin. Its carried offering drops and an unwitnessed hold is voided.
  • ALL-STOP freezes the haunt for everyone and cannot be undone locally.

Items

Every item has a role: usable, ambiance, or hazard-cover.

Professions

Players pick a profession with tiered passives and actions. Only part of the roster is playable; the roadmap lists what is left.
