## Scope

Behrouz, Hashemi, Javanmard and Mirrokni. Submitted June 2, revised July 10, 2026. The 10M-token result uses Hope on BABILong with benchmark fine-tuning; the appendix reports substantial degradation without it. Table 2 gives Qwen3-8B average@16 scores: base 73.8/68.1/42.4 and Sleep 79.2/69.0/46.1 on AIME24/AIME25/HMMT25. Experimental setups differ. Authors report gains; HIVE has not reproduced them.
