**Parent topic:** [Memory, evidence, and knowledge graphs](T05_Memory.md)

## Question

How should HIVE retain two claims that disagree?

## Retained branches

| Branch | Candidate mechanism | Current status |

| P17.A | Separate claims with scope and conditions | Retained for comparison |
| P17.B | One preferred conclusion plus a preserved challenger | Retained for comparison |

## Prototype to build

Store claim, source, exact locator, date, method, population or task, measured outcome, and inference separately. Link supports, challenges, and different-scope relationships.

## Measurement and failure condition

Check that a later summary still exposes contrary evidence. Repetition by multiple agents does not create independent corroboration.
