# Source and claim template

## Required fields

Canonical source URL; author/project; publication and version dates; retrieved date; exact locator; scoped proposition; observation versus inference; supporting and challenging evidence; code/data availability; local replication status.

## Completion rule

Use exact identifiers and measured values. Mark unavailable information explicitly. A proposed run has no fabricated timestamp, score, or completion status.

## Links to add

Parent topic; relevant prior method; supporting source; alternative or disputed path; experiment or decision; next research avenue.
