# DeepMind AlphaFold 2/3

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Specialist/Adapter  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

Google DeepMind's AI system that predicts 3D protein structures from amino-acid sequence (AlphaFold 2) and, in AlphaFold 3, the joint structures of proteins with DNA, RNA, small-molecule ligands and ions.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy AlphaFold's narrow, benchmark-verifiable specialist scope with confidence scores and a free, reusable artifact/database that creates adoption, and avoid gating the core capability for a commercial spinout or letting a flagship specialist team be absorbed into a generalist product line.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
