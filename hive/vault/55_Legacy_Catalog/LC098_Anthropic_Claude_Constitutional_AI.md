# Anthropic Claude / Constitutional AI

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Memory/Verification/Self-improvement  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

Claude is Anthropic's single-model AI assistant family, trained with Constitutional AI, in which the model critiques and revises its own behaviour against an explicit written constitution instead of relying only on human preference labels.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy an explicit, written constitution plus self-critique and verification gates for agents, and avoid depending on human feedback alone or on multi-agent coordination overhead to enforce behaviour.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
