# SpiNNaker / SpiNNaker2

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Bio-inspired/Neuromorphic/Swarm  
**Original catalog label:** In Progress — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

A massively parallel, brain-inspired neuromorphic supercomputer architecture from the University of Manchester (SpiNNaker1, 1M ARM cores) and its commercial second generation SpiNNaker2 from TU Dresden spinout SpiNNcloud, using many small event-driven cores to simulate spiking neural networks.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy its event-driven, many-small-cores sparsity model and its research-lab-first adoption path, but avoid betting HIVE on neuromorphic hardware until it has proven, independently benchmarked wins on production AI workloads rather than neuroscience simulations.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
