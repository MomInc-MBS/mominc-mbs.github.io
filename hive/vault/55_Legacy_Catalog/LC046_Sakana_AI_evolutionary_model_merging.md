# Sakana AI evolutionary model merging

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Bio-inspired/Neuromorphic/Swarm  
**Original catalog label:** In Progress — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

An evolutionary method that automatically searches parameter-space (weights) and data-flow-space (layers) combinations of existing open-source specialist models to create new merged foundation models without gradient-based training.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy fitness-driven merging of specialist models/adapters with explicit evaluation as a cheap capability-composition layer, but avoid making merging the product itself — it only works on open-weight models and Sakana has moved the same evolutionary idea up to inference-time orchestration.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
