# Palantir AIP (Artificial Intelligence Platform)

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Orchestration/Enterprise  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

Palantir's enterprise generative-AI platform that connects LLMs and AI agents to an organization's live data, workflows and operations through its Ontology, with governance, evals and human oversight.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy AIP's Ontology-first grounding — agents act only on a governed, permissioned model of data, decisions and actions with audit trails and evals — and avoid its heavyweight, high-touch bootcamp deployment model.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
