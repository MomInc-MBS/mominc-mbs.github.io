# IBM Watson (Jeopardy / Watson Health)

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Orchestration/Enterprise  
**Original catalog label:** Failed — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

IBM Watson was a DeepQA natural-language question-answering system that beat Jeopardy! champions in 2011 and was then commercialised as Watson Health, an enterprise clinical-decision-support and health-data platform.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy Watson's narrow, measurable demo discipline, but avoid HIVE overselling a benchmark win as general competence — validate on real workflows with real data and safety checks before marketing or clinical/high-stakes claims.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
