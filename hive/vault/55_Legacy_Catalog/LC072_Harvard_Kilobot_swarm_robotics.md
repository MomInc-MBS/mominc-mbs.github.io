# Harvard Kilobot swarm robotics

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Bio-inspired/Neuromorphic/Swarm  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

A Harvard (Radhika Nagpal lab) swarm of 1,024 tiny, ~$15, 3.3 cm robots that self-assemble into shapes using only simple local rules and infrared neighbor communication, with no central control.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy Kilobot's formula: many cheap, simple, homogeneous agents with local neighbor-only communication, self-healing/error-correction rules, and no central controller — and avoid central orchestration becoming the scaling bottleneck.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
