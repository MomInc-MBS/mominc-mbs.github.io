# Salesforce Agentforce

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Orchestration/Enterprise  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

Salesforce's enterprise platform for building, deploying, and governing autonomous AI agents that act on CRM and business data across service, sales, marketing, and operations.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy Agentforce's formula of grounding agents in a trusted data layer, bounding autonomy with explicit permissions and guardrails, and shipping observability/testing from the start; avoid selling unbounded autonomy before production retention and deep adoption are proven.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
