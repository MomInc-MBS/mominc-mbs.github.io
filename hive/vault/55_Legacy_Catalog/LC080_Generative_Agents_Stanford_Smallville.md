# Generative Agents / Stanford Smallville

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Multi-agent framework  
**Original catalog label:** Succeeded — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

A 2023 Stanford/Google research system in which 25 LLM-driven agents live in a Sims-like town called Smallville, remembering, reflecting on, and planning their days, producing emergent social behaviour such as organising a Valentine's Day party from a single seed instruction.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy the memory-stream + reflection + hierarchical-planning architecture with recency/importance/relevance retrieval, and ground agents in real interview/data rather than fictional personas; avoid shipping the unmaintained Smallville demo as-is, given its high token cost, retrieval failures, and lack of long-run coherence.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
