# BrainScaleS-2 (Heidelberg)

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Bio-inspired/Neuromorphic/Swarm  
**Original catalog label:** In Progress — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

An analog mixed-signal neuromorphic research platform from Heidelberg University's Electronic Vision(s) group that emulates spiking neural networks in physical circuits 1,000x faster than biology, freely accessible to researchers via the EBRAINS infrastructure.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy its shared-infrastructure playbook — standard PyTorch/PyNN interfaces, CI/CD-tested hardware access, and free researcher onboarding that turned scarce exotic hardware into a 520-user platform — but avoid its grant-dependent, analog-hardware-bound model: build on commodity digital infrastructure HIVE can actually scale and sustain commercially.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
