# Cortical Labs DishBrain

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Bio-inspired/Neuromorphic/Swarm  
**Original catalog label:** In Progress — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

DishBrain is Cortical Labs' 2022 system of ~800,000 living human and mouse cortical neurons grown on a microelectrode array and taught to play Pong via closed-loop electrical feedback, later commercialised as the CL1 biological computer.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy DishBrain's closed-loop, embodied feedback learning — small adaptive units improving from success/failure signals with minimal energy — but avoid building HIVE on a fragile, short-lived substrate or unreplicated claims; require independent benchmarks before scaling any bio-inspired mechanism.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
