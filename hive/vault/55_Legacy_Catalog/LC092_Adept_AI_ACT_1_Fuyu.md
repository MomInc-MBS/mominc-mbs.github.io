# Adept AI (ACT-1 / Fuyu)

**Status:** historical import; current project condition and numerical claims are not reverified here.  
**Original catalog category:** Orchestration/Enterprise  
**Original catalog label:** Stalled/Abandoned — a prior classification, not this plan's experimental verdict.

## Prior description

Adept AI built ACT-1, an Action Transformer that observes screens and operates software via clicks and keystrokes, and the Fuyu family of multimodal models optimized for UI understanding.

## Research lead carried forward

Copy Fuyu's radically simple encoder-free screen-grounding for computer-use agents, but avoid Adept's fatal split — never fund a frontier foundation model and an enterprise product at the same time without a shipped, paying vertical first.

This is a historical suggestion to investigate. Adoption requires current primary evidence, a working interface, and a workload comparison.
