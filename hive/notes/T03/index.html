Coordination and supervision

Central question

How should specialists assemble, recruit, recover, and change their connections?

Separate research paths

Path Question Main experiment
Controller and routing Which decisions require a learned controller? Compare generalists, independent specialists, adapters and hybrids · Use measured interference to test regrouping and routing
Communities and local recruitment Can local coordination reduce central monitoring? Scale 100, 1,000 and 10,000 logical experts with measured active workers · Compare whole-system and per-specialist sleep during real service
Adaptive graph and grouping Should HIVE change connections and specialist boundaries? Use measured interference to test regrouping and routing · Scale 100, 1,000 and 10,000 logical experts with measured active workers
Local supervisors and intervention learning How does a repeated human fix become a dependable local capability? Measure the actual computer and concurrent workload · Compare whole-system and per-specialist sleep during real service
