Immune discrimination and memory admission

  • Observed precedent: Forrest and colleagues proposed immune-inspired change detection, analyzed its computational costs, and reported preliminary computer experiments; this is an algorithmic analogy, not HIVE validation.
  • Software translation — proposal: Give incoming facts, tools, and training examples explicit provenance and admission rules. Schema checks, executable verification, source identity, and contradiction records act as separate detectors. Uncertain material enters a reviewable holding area.
  • Competing branches: Deterministic admission; learned anomaly scoring; independent detector combinations; permissive storage with restricted use. Compare detector diversity with repeated copies of the same critic.
  • Falsifiable experiment: Introduce known false facts, changed units, altered records, duplicate evidence, and unfamiliar correct facts. Measure wrong information accepted and valid novelty rejected. Reject an admission policy that merely suppresses novelty without improving downstream correctness.
  • Analogy limit: Unfamiliar does not mean harmful or false. Different internal languages do not enforce isolation; scoped access and message validation must do that work.
  • Adjacent avenues: Type systems, provenance graphs, anomaly detection, capability security, and error-correcting representations.
