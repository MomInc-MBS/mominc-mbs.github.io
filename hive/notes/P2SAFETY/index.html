P2 safety and objective campaign - dev6

The next genuine acceptance campaign uses six structured-data tasks plus six Python tasks. Ian confirmed that the newer objective-only plan supersedes the older video/code wording in the attached handoff.

Verified engineering

P2 v4 requires a permitted exact tool or abstention for exact tasks. Typed contracts bind source, permission, verifier and resource identities; isolated feedback cannot promote itself into the frozen governing policy. Replay checks saved configuration and feedback. Interrupted verified feedback can complete without redispatch or duplicate accounting while resources remain.

448 tests passed, 2 explained Windows symlink skips and zero failures; seven CLI journeys passed. The twelve public objective tasks passed exact checks and fresh-process unchanged-budget replay. Six Python tasks actually ran in the pinned Docker worker. These are exposed fixed development examples, not fresh acceptance or autonomous solving.

P2 remains quarantined. No policy promotion or learning gain is claimed. Specialist/model-team comparisons require a new finite allocation. The old 30-call and six-call ledgers remain spent and unchanged.

Failures and limits

Independent review exposed feedback/configuration binding, legacy rollback and interrupted-feedback defects; the final source repairs them and retains the original probes. The baseline's first CLI run had one timeout while tests ran concurrently; its one-case serial retry passed. The final candidate's seven journeys ran serially. Prior dev4/dev5 failures remain in their original records.

STOP and resource ceilings still take precedence: after interruption at an exhausted cap, the job stays queued for operator reconciliation. Historical skill-aggregate reconstruction, cross-source transfer, CPU/GPU utilization and separate controller/verifier costs are not established. The bounded Grok review and Terra's independent checks state their own scope in the linked verdict.

The next real campaign still needs twelve approved unused sources, private independent verifiers and source-specific finite resources. Public examples cannot be relabeled as unused acceptance data. No editorial review is needed for the confirmed objective-only campaign.
