Previous research and attempted methods

What was recovered

The package preserves 19 original project artifacts, a linked history page for each source document, 12 method-history pages, and navigable entries from the prior 100-project catalog. See Recovered original documents and Prior 100-project research catalog.

What the artifact audit establishes

  • 32 archived experiment manifest entries matched recorded file hashes and byte counts.
  • Raw records document 360 small NumPy learning runs and 1,440 scheduling simulations.
  • Central statistics were recalculated from the recorded JSON.
  • The experiment training scripts were not rerun during this preparation.
  • These artifacts do not document real-model tests on the user's RTX 5070 Ti.

Findings that affect the new plan

Prior result Implication Follow-up
Conflicting tasks: mixed two-expert grouping 66.11%, compatible grouping 83.88% Organization deserves direct testing Specialist grouping, sharing, and interference → Use measured interference to test regrouping and routing
Mostly shared tasks: generalist 91.03%, isolated experts 87.00% Sharing can help; isolation is not a universal winner Specialist grouping, sharing, and interference → Independent models or shared components
Conflicting generalist: interleaved replay 74.47%, blocked replay 62.64% Keep the negative scheduling result within its tested scope Replay ordering and the reopened sleep branch → Test sleep, replay order, expansion and distillation independently
Precise prose and precise numerical formats preserved all tested geometric labels Precision and representation are separate variables Representation precision and communication cost → Compare typed messages, precise prose and learned representations
A stale translator lost accuracy after encoder drift Version bridges and test consumers together Private representations and translator drift → Learned codes and translators
Well-formed semantic errors passed typed gates Syntax and authority checks do not establish truth Typed messages, authority, and semantic errors → Executable verification
Scheduler studies used 4/8/16 workers Larger registry scales remain new experiments Model size, resource budgets, and larger hive scales → Scale 100, 1,000 and 10,000 logical experts with measured active workers

All percentages are historical synthetic-task outcomes. Conditions and limitations are on the linked pages and in the original report.
