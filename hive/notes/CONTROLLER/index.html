Tests and defects resolved

122 runtime tests passed. Two Tester Army tools-only CLI journeys passed, including STOP → resume → completed replay without spending more budget. The exporter passed 10 tests; one filesystem symlink test was skipped because Windows did not permit creating the test symlink.

The initial STOP CLI failure is retained in experiments/results/adaptive-e2e-initial-failure.json. Fixed defects include ordinary STOP returning an error, wrong-job evidence substitution, unchecked cached evidence on partial resume, and a process-death window splitting an incumbent change from its decision. Decisions now commit atomically and reproduce from verified evidence on resume. Tests inject death at that write boundary, test a real child-process exit, corrupt evidence, alter plans and exhaust budgets.

Research and limits

Sol implemented paired descriptive assessment; Luna implemented the controller; Terra tested recovery and adversarial cases. NVIDIA Nemotron reviewed the statistical plan. Its useful challenge removed utility-only acceptance: quality must improve strictly without critical regression. Utility and family dispersion are descriptive. The NVIDIA review and dispositions are saved with the results.

Repeated validation is candidate-selection evidence, not an unused holdout. Six correlated synthetic families and one model cannot demonstrate generalization, compounding learning, weight training or superiority over the strongest simple baseline. Deterministic tools won this run. Their measured zero latency is below timer resolution, not zero physical compute. A candidate's acceptance is only experimental; it cannot approve its own production checkpoint.

The next research bottleneck is a preregistered suite with unseen families and tasks on which neither simple route saturates. Repeated inference and negative controls must precede any broad gain claim. Model output still cannot execute arbitrary code, change evaluators, or expand resource limits.
