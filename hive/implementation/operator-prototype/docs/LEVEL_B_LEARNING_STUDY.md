# Level B learning study: untested

**UNTESTED; no inference or promotion authorized.** The plan interface remains
`experiments/plans/readiness-level-b.json`. Complete Level A first and obtain a
separate approved study allocation. No development fixture or exposed Level A
answer may be relabeled as sealed Level B material.

Before any study exposure, the independent evaluation owner must freeze:

- At least 24 previously unused, source-disjoint matched tasks, with two
  chronological waves for new-source transfer and older-family retention.
- Exact task contracts, source split/exposure ledger, private evaluator/gold
  identities, model/runtime/policy hashes and matched finite budgets.
- The strongest applicable unchanged deterministic control, best fixed
  specialist, fixed two-component team, learning-disabled controller and candidate.
- A practically meaningful quality margin, source-group uncertainty procedure,
  sufficient sample size and critical-regression/retention thresholds. The
  handoff's 10 percentage-point margin is illustrative, not a retrospectively
  adopted passing rule. Twenty-four tasks may be insufficient.
- Memory, routing, collaboration and consolidation ablations with full charged
  attempts, retries, verifier overhead and failed work. A verifier-off arm is a
  negative control and cannot produce verified acceptance.

P2 v4's conservative memory partition permits only matching source, split,
permission, verifier and executable identity. This establishes an isolation
boundary; cross-source transfer has not been implemented or demonstrated by
that boundary. A later training-scope change needs its own development hypothesis
and frozen study design before sealed exposure.

Promotion requires independently verified quality gain over the strongest
applicable control, no preregistered critical failure, acceptable retention,
respected authority/budgets, replayable receipts and explicit human approval.
All other outcomes remain REJECT, QUARANTINE or INCONCLUSIVE. Cost savings,
route changes, safety abstentions and passing synthetic tests are not substitutes
for a measured useful-learning gain. Model weights remain unchanged.
