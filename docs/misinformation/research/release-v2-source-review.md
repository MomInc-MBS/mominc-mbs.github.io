## Science classification and scope review

The science file contains 56 distinct answer subjects: 40 documented-event/object/program propositions and 16 unsupported or contradicted propositions. Classification applies only to the displayed statement. A true astronomy fact about Mars must not inherit the false label of the separate Mars-size rumor. The two hidden-capability allegations marked unsupported are not presented as experimentally proven nonexistence.

The reviewed wording preserves these distinctions:

- [CDC's archived 2021 fact sheet](https://stacks.cdc.gov/view/cdc/109015/cdc_109015_DS1.pdf) is used for the dated microchip/magnetism claims and the specifically mRNA DNA-rewrite proposition. The text does not generalize an mRNA mechanism to every vaccine platform.
- [NOAA's weather-modification fact check](https://www.noaa.gov/news/fact-check-debunking-weather-modification-claims) distinguishes forecasting from hurricane creation/steering; this does not deny documented historical weather experiments.
- [NASA's solar-energy comparison](https://science.nasa.gov/climate-change/faq/is-the-sun-causing-global-warming/) concerns the post-1950s warming trend, not a denial that the Sun influences climate.
- [USGS earthquake-weather](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/there-earthquake-weather) and [magnetic-reversal](https://www.usgs.gov/faqs/do-any-mass-extinctions-correlate-magnetic-reversals) accounts are kept narrow: reliable weather prediction and an alleged universal extinction connection. The cards do not deny all small pressure effects or every possible consequence of field variation.
- Voyager 1 is described as entering interstellar space, not escaping all solar gravitational influence. DART changed Dimorphos's orbit about Didymos, not the trajectory of an asteroid threatening Earth.

Some false-prediction and science-rumor cards do not allege a secret coordinated plot. Their categories say unsupported science/sky/prediction claim. Broad game wording should use unsupported claim when including those cards, rather than teaching that every error is a conspiracy theory. Familiarity ratings are editorial estimates, not observed population rarity.
