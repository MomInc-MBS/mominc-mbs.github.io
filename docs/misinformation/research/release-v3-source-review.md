# Version-three answer-bank source review

Reviewed 2026-10-15. Release target and implementation checks are in [RELEASE.md](../RELEASE.md). This review covers the new answer-bank inputs and aliases; hosted retrieval is a separate verification path, documented in the [judge README](../../../backend/misinformation-judge/README.md).

The original authored [49-card expansion](../research-data/expansion-v3-world-events.json) contributes **43 documented propositions and six unsupported or contradicted propositions**, with 48 distinct institutional source URLs. Forty-seven answers have editorial familiarity 0 or 1. The combined reviewed bank contains 204 distinct answers, 170 documented propositions and 34 claim propositions. Familiarity estimates are not measured population rarity, and truth labels apply only to the displayed statement.

Sources were checked through institutional primary histories, scientific-agency explanations, treaty records and organizations' own founding histories. Cards use original concise wording and links. Linked prose, photographs and media are not republished; no blanket reuse license is asserted. The complete card-to-source mapping remains in the JSON rather than being duplicated in this report.

## Aliases and matching checks

The existing [Cuban Missile Crisis source card](../research-data/expansion-v2-history.json) retains its [State Department diplomatic-history source](https://history.state.gov/milestones/1961-1968/cuban-missile-crisis) and exact October 1962 proposition. Added aliases include `cuban missle crisis`, `cuban missles crisis`, `cuban missil crisis`, `cuban missile crises`, `cuba missile crisis`, `cuban missile crisis 1962` and `october crisis 1962`. They are spelling/name aliases of one canonical answer, not additional answers or evidence of a different event.

The new bank adds familiar abbreviations and alternate names such as WWI/WW1, WWII/WW2, Great Recession, Boxing Day tsunami and Desert Storm. Engine normalization removes case, accents, punctuation and spaces; it does not promise arbitrary typo correction. Explicit aliases and the separately hosted fit check address wider matching.

Thirteen catalog/expansion tests passed during this task. They verify sourced required fields, collision-free normalized aliases, semantic scopes, a common-answer majority, minimum seventeen prompt memberships, and familiar world-event lookup across five broad prompts, including Ian's Cuban misspelling. They do not certify every possible user answer or model decision. No further high-impact factual correction was identified in this bounded source pass. The earlier Saddam source replacement remains preserved in the authoritative version-two input.

The separate twelve-template ad-lib library is original fictional office/space wordplay, not another factual source corpus. Seven story tests cover role-compatible prefill, honest empty fields, unknown-name handling, bounded plain text and separation of a case from a person.
