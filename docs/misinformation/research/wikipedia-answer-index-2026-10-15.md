# Wikipedia answer index — 2026-10-15

The generated [index](../research-data/wikipedia-index.json) contains **5,072 distinct English Wikipedia articles** and **26,709 redirect aliases**. The bounded importer visited 300 categories, at most five levels deep, and found 5,389 main-namespace article IDs; 259 without a usable short extract and 58 entries with only excluded Hoaxers paths did not enter the final index. Source roots cover conspiracy theories, urban legends, pseudoscience, hoaxes, political scandals, intelligence and false flag operations, human research, assassinations, and the Apollo program. People, fiction, media, and works category branches are excluded from traversal. A path records why an article was found; membership alone does not label its subject as a false claim.

**519 articles have an explicit topic label**, with 527 labels total because some subjects span types: 160 conspiracy theories, 123 hoaxes, 148 pseudoscience topics, and 96 urban legends. Labels come from the article title or intro, not blanket category membership. A bounded longer-lead check corrected one missed name, **Dead Internet theory**, after its 25-word card excerpt ended before the article's conspiracy-theory identification. The index stores only the derived label, source revision ID, and check timestamp for that correction. Other similarly named articles describing collections, historical plots, scientific theories, people, or books were not relabeled.

Every exported article has a measured `views` number from English Wikipedia's [PageViewInfo Action API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Extension:PageViewInfo#API), which exposes Wikimedia pageview data. Values sum numeric daily observations over **2026-09-15 through 2026-10-14 UTC** (`pvipdays=30`). A null day is omitted from the sum; if a page has no numeric daily observations, its `views` is null and must be treated as **unrated**, never rare. This run exported 5,072 measured counts and zero null article counts. Views are a rough public familiarity proxy, not observed game answer frequency. The importer limits requests to two concurrent 10-ID batches, handles API continuation and retries, and checkpoints in the system temp directory for resumption.

Article IDs, category paths, redirects, intro extracts, and revision IDs come from the [MediaWiki Action API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Categorymembers), [redirects API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/API:Redirects), and [TextExtracts API](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Extension:TextExtracts). Each `description` contains at most the first 25 words of the article's intro. Each record links to its article and carries the revision and **Wikipedia contributors (CC BY-SA 4.0)** attribution with the [license URL](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). See [Wikimedia's reuse guidance](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Legal:Wikimedia_Developer_App_Guidelines) for attribution and share-alike terms.

Regenerate with `python tools/import_misinformation_wikipedia.py`; the importer writes only this index and its external temporary checkpoint.
