## Runtime requirements that follow from the textures
All `textures/*.png` are POT, uploaded RGBA8, `REPEAT`, `LINEAR` + generated mipmaps (`LINEAR_MIPMAP_LINEAR`). The hatch/paper looks alias without mipmaps.

## Reviewed but not used (see docs/material_worlds/research.md)
photo-art-filters-android shaders are `#version 300 es` and depend on a mipmapped `textureLod` blur prelude (`glsl/prelude.glsl`), so none are portable to GLES2/WebGL1 as written; ideas were reused, code was not. GPUImage shaders are GLES2 but use their own varyings/uniform names; only the Sobel kernel was reused.
