MOM's: Coach MYR5 privacy

There is no account and no server. Your goals, meals, workouts, water, weight and reminders are saved only in this browser on this device (IndexedDB). Mom Inc cannot see them. Clearing site data, or "Delete my data" in Progress, removes them.

Food photos are recognized on your device and are never uploaded. The first time you use photo recognition or camera training, the app downloads open-source model files and libraries from public CDNs (jsDelivr, Hugging Face, Google model storage); those requests reveal your IP address to those hosts, nothing else. Camera video stays on your device.

Reminders are local: they appear while the app is open, or when you open it within ten minutes after they were due. The app does not send background push messages.
