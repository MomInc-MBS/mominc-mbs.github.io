MYR5 vs Zombies, Run!

Last checked: 6 October 2026. Written by MYR5, the maker of MYR5.

Pick Zombies, Run! if you want to run or walk outside (or on a treadmill) and be pulled along by a story. Pick MYR5 if you want a free indoor bodyweight session where your phone camera counts your reps. Both use fiction as motivation; they suit different workouts.

What Zombies, Run! does better

  • Depth of content: 500+ story missions, against MYR5's 57 camera-tracked movements, which have no story missions.[1]
  • A track record: its App Store listing shows a 4.8 rating from more than 24,000 ratings. MYR5 has no reviews yet.[1]
  • It gets you outside and works on a treadmill, and includes a Couch to 5K plan.[1]
  • It works with your own music and with Apple Watch.[1]

What MYR5 does differently

  • It is free, with no subscription.
  • The phone camera counts reps and estimates holds, so you get feedback on each set. The camera and pose processing stay on your phone.
  • It runs in the browser, so there is nothing to download from a store.
  • It is for ages 13 and up.

Who should choose which

  • You like running, or want to start: Zombies, Run!
  • You want a short strength session at home and like having your reps counted: MYR5.
  • You want both: they do not overlap much, so using both is reasonable.

FAQ

Is Zombies, Run! free?

The download is free and includes Season 1. Full access is $6.99 a month or $49.99 a year, according to its App Store listing.[1]

Is MYR5 free?

Yes. MYR5 is free and runs in a phone browser.

Can I use MYR5 on a run?

No. MYR5 is for indoor bodyweight exercise in front of a propped-up phone camera. For running, Zombies, Run! is the better choice.
