MYR5 vs Nike Training Club

Last checked: 6 October 2026. Written by MYR5, the maker of MYR5.

Both are free. Pick Nike Training Club if you want a large library of professional, trainer-led workouts and multi-week plans. Pick MYR5 if a normal workout app has not stuck for you and you want a game-like coach that counts your reps with the phone camera.

What Nike Training Club does better

  • Professional coaching: workouts are led by Nike Master Trainers. MYR5 has no professional trainers.[1]
  • Volume and range: 200+ workouts covering many lengths, with and without equipment.[1]
  • Planning: 4 to 6 week plans that adjust to your progress and schedule.[1]
  • Native apps, including Apple Watch, from a brand with a long track record.[1]

What MYR5 does differently

  • The phone camera counts reps and estimates holds, so the app reacts to what you actually did. The camera and pose processing stay on your phone.
  • Workouts are wrapped in a game: a customizable alien coach, with progress earned by finished sets.
  • It runs in the browser, with nothing to install from a store. For ages 13 and up.

Who should choose which

  • You want structured training from professionals: Nike Training Club.
  • You get bored of follow-along videos and like games: MYR5.
  • You have weights or a gym: Nike Training Club, which has equipment workouts.[1]

FAQ

Is Nike Training Club free?

Yes. Nike says the NTC app is free to download and offers 200+ free workouts.[1]

Does Nike Training Club count my reps?

NTC workouts are trainer-led sessions you follow along with. Nike's app page does not describe camera rep counting. MYR5 counts reps with the phone camera.

Does MYR5 upload my camera video?

No. MYR5's camera and pose processing stay on your phone.
