Install and map your room

  1. Download and install the APK on your Android phone. It updates Haunt v5 or v4.
  2. Keep Room Mapper running on the PC and put the phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Scan the PC's room QR, or open your private phone link and tap Open in Haunt app.
  4. Tap Record video or Pick video. Walk slowly and capture overlapping room views.
  5. Tap Upload & build map. When ready, view the map in the app and use Share 3D file to export it.

The PC reconstructs your recordings locally. Up to three videos per room, 512 MiB each. Geometry and measurements are estimates. Physical-phone room accuracy still needs verification.

New in v6: location test

Test live phone tracking, shared room markers, optional GPS zones, location cues, and ALL STOP with the updated PC Room Mapper.

  1. After building a room map, tap Track this phone in room in the app and allow camera access.
  2. Look around and slowly move sideways so the PC can align the phone with the room map.
  3. GPS starts off. Turn on GPS location and allow location access only if you want to test GPS-area cues.
  4. Stop tracking releases camera and GPS. ALL STOP ends the shared room session.

This is a development test build. Physical-phone alignment and camera orientation still need verification. Location cues currently display named events; full haunt integration is still pending.
