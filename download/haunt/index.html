Install and map your room

  1. Download and install the APK on your Android phone. It updates Haunt v4.
  2. Keep Room Mapper running on the PC and put the phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  3. Scan the PC's room QR, or open your private phone link and tap Open in Haunt app.
  4. Tap Record video or Pick video. Walk slowly and capture overlapping room views.
  5. Tap Upload & build map. When ready, view the map in the app and use Share 3D file to export it.

The PC reconstructs your recordings locally. Up to three videos per room, 512 MiB each. Geometry and measurements are estimates. Physical-phone room accuracy still needs verification.
