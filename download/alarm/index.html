How it works

  • Start is free play on the 2048 board. It never touches your alarm.
  • Set alarm picks one daily time, in your phone's own time zone. That is the only setting.
  • At that time the app posts local notifications about every 30 seconds (Android may delay or group them) until you merge a genuine 2048 tile. Tapping one opens the board. Losing deals a fresh board for the same alarm.
  • It asks for notification access and, on some Android versions, permission for exact alarms. Without them it tells you it is not armed.
  • No account, no internet permission, nothing leaves the phone. Force Stop or uninstall switches it off, because Android says so.

Install: open the downloaded file and allow installs from this source when Android asks. This is a development build signed with a debug key, so it cannot update a future release build.
