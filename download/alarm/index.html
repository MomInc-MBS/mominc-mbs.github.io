ADHD — Alarm for the Distracted, Hungry & Disorganized

An alarm with four horrible horns (Air Horn, Train Horn, Industrial Buzzer, Crash Alarm) that keeps after you until you merge a real 2048 tile, inside the Coach Reminders mini-computer.

How it works

  • Start is free play on the 2048 board. It never touches your alarm.
  • Set alarm: pick a time, an optional task (like "Eat breakfast") and one of four intense sounds. Tap ▶ to hear one first. Nothing is armed until you confirm Arm this alarm, and every change asks again.
  • At that time the phone rings with your sound on the alarm channel until you merge a genuine 2048 tile. With a task you also tap Task done. Losing deals a fresh board for the same alarm.
  • Opt in to follow-ups and they escalate at 30 s, 90 s, 3 min and 5 min: “Still waiting for your check-off.” Swiping a notice away never counts as done.
  • Stop alarm always works. It ends today's alarm as not done; tomorrow's stays set.
  • It asks for notification access and, on some Android versions, exact alarms and full-screen alarms. Without them it tells you it is not armed.
  • No account, no internet permission, nothing leaves the phone. Force Stop or uninstall switches it off, because Android says so.

Install: open the downloaded file and allow installs from this source when Android asks. Already have 0.1.0? This installs over it and keeps your alarm time; open Set alarm once to pick a sound. This is a development build signed with a debug key, so it cannot update a future release build.
