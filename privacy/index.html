Publishing App Privacy Notice

Current as of October 13, 2026

This notice describes the MOM Inc. self-hosted publishing workflow used to prepare and publish content to MOM Inc.'s own platform accounts. It covers that publishing workflow, not the separate privacy practices of each social platform.

Information handled by the publishing workflow

The local Postiz service stores the platform account authorization used to connect an account, original media files prepared for posts, and post drafts and approved content. The workflow uses this information to manage the selected account and prepare its content for publication.

Review and publication

Posts require operator review and approval. An operator manually initiates publication of approved content to the selected platform. The workflow does not currently collect audience data or monitor comments.

Storage and sharing

The publisher runs locally on the operator-controlled machine. Its Postiz interface is bound to the local device, and the connected account authorization and original media are stored by that local service. When the operator publishes a post, the selected platform receives the content and media needed for that post and processes them under its own terms and privacy notice.

MOM Inc. does not sell the data handled by this publishing workflow.

Revoking access or requesting deletion

The platform account owner can revoke the publishing service's authorization through the platform's account or security settings. To request that MOM Inc. disconnect an account or remove the account authorization, draft content, or media held in the local publishing service, contact mominc.stationcentral@gmail.com.

Removing local copies or revoking authorization does not remove content already published or retained by a platform. Requests to remove published content should also be made through that platform.

Contact

For privacy questions or account access and deletion requests about this publishing workflow, email mominc.stationcentral@gmail.com.
