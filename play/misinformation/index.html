      THE EXIT PROCEDURE

      Uncommon knowledge.
      Very common danger.

      1. Name something that fits.You have 35 seconds. Familiar answers count; rarity earns 10–100 points. Unlisted names get an evidence-backed hosted check, with your timer paused.
      2. Read the exact statement.Is it documented, or an unsupported claim? A wrong call damages your hull. An article about a theory does not make the theory true.
      3. Escape through 20 paper worlds.Five questions maximum. Only five correctly classified extra rare Motherlode answers reach the desk. Lower tiers travel less; passes and timeouts use a question. Three hits end the run early. Turn your flight's answers into an ad-lib incident report.
      THE DEEPER YOU GO, THE STRANGER IT GETS.

      Ten broad prompts in each topic. The office gives way to space, paper tears, The Fold, and full dimensions. Familiar answers are plentiful, and missed transmissions unlock a high-scoring example. Conspiracy-focused transmissions arrive deeper into the run. Unused questions and topics rotate before repeats, including across restarts. Every resolved answer moves the chase. Reviewed rarity tiers are editorial. Imported topics use Wikipedia readership as a familiarity proxy, not player frequency. Newly verified online answers receive 15 points of discovery credit while their rarity remains unrated.

      Includes non-graphic references to historical atrocities, disasters, abuse, and serial murder. Scores reward recall, never harm. Grade the exact statement: a real investigation does not prove every theory about it. The hosted model judges prompt fit; the linked evidence supplies the fact card. It can make mistakes.
